![Godot](https://img.shields.io/badge/Godot-4.x-478CBF?logo=godot-engine&logoColor=white)
![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-MIT-grean.svg)

A 2D side-view where the cloud gives bread and hailstone, move the basket to catch the bread dodge the stones before you break the basket
---



## features
- Responsive movement
- Randomized movement and timing
- Sound effects and music
- Cute art 😅
